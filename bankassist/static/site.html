What it will not do

The section your risk officer
reads first.

An assistant that occasionally invents an interest rate is not a cheaper contact centre — it is a regulatory incident with a chat interface. These are enforced in code and checked by the test suite on every change, not written into a prompt and hoped for.

01

It cannot invent a rate, a fee, or a balance

Figures come from retrieved content or from a tool, never from the model's own memory. An evaluation suite runs against a fixed set of questions before any knowledge-base or model change ships.

02

It refuses anything needing account access — and is tested for over-refusing too

Balances, PINs, card blocks and transfers are out of bounds without an authenticated session. Checked in both directions: an assistant that refuses "what documents do I need to open an account?" has failed just as surely as one that leaks a balance.

03

Education, not advice

It explains how a product works. It does not tell a customer which one to buy. That line is in the contract as well as the code.

04

One institution's data cannot reach another's

Every query touching tenant data is scoped by institution, and an integration test asserts it.

05

Chat text is never logged, and every staff action is

Message content stays out of application logs. What is recorded is what the system did — actor, action, entity. Administrator accounts carry a second factor.

Pricing

Per institution. Never per seat.

Charging per agent seat punishes you for putting the assistant in front of more customers, which is the entire point of buying it. So the model is a one-time setup fee plus a monthly subscription tiered by conversation volume — the shape a bank's procurement team can actually approve.

Pilot

One institution, one or two channels, a fixed term
  • Your published content imported and answering
  • Website widget and Telegram
  • Both reports, with export
  • A success measure agreed before it starts

Institution

A bank or MFI in production
  • All seven channels
  • Live teller handoff, desks and routing
  • Six languages, your branding on the widget
  • In-country deployment
  • Evaluation suite run before every change

Group

A holding company or several institutions
  • Multiple tenants, strictly isolated
  • Per-institution knowledge bases and branding
  • Consolidated reporting
  • Integration with existing contact-centre tooling
We publish the model, not a number. What it costs depends on your conversation volume, which channels you switch on, and whether the deployment is in-country from day one. Tell us those three things and you get a written price — not a range that changes when you ask twice.
